| | |
|---|---|

| **Where** | Bubblehouse PE Party Garden, Plot 8, Farm 23, Kraggakamma Drive |
| **Tickets** | Live from **Monday 10 August**. Sold out fast last year, per the organiser |
| **Source** | [@bubblehouse_portelizabeth on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/bubblehouse_portelizabeth/) |
| **Price** | Not announced yet. Confirm before it runs |

Billed as PE's biggest kids' festival. Separated age groups covering ages 1 to 13,
new inflatables, and a long vendor line-up: Danny-Go dance along, Disco Dad, a
Busy Tots giant bubble show, face painting, pony rides, a playdough station, a
braid bar, and a cap-decorating stand from Playhouse PE.

**Run it in #021 (13 August), not later.** Tickets open the Monday before, so the
13th is the first issue that can carry a live booking link while the urgency is
real. Leaving it to #022 wastes the "before they're gone" angle entirely.

**Honest read against the playbook.** Kids-specific events are our worst-performing
category: 2 clicks or fewer, every time. This one has a genuine case for being the
exception, but do not mistake the exception for a rule.

- It is a *festival* with a dozen food vendors, which is category 1 stacked on
  category 3, our two strongest.
- Tickets that actually sell out give a real reason to click *now*, which is the
  entire job of the Save The Date slot.
- The audience is narrow either way. Parents of under-13s are a slice of the list,
  not the list.

**So: Save The Date, not Editor's Pick.** The Pick has to be broadly appealing, and
this is not. Track it and settle the question with data: if it clears 5 clicks it
justifies revisiting the kids-category rule; if it lands at 2 like everything else
in the category, that rule is confirmed and this stops being a debate.

food businesses in one place, all already paying for visibility: The Mustard Seed,
The Sweet Cart PE, Wayward Girls, Jack's Bagels Summerstrand, Chip n Dip GQ,
Parties by Ilke, Lizzy's Gourmet Ice Cream, My Artisan Cart, Emile's Roosterkoek,
Taqwa Coffee Shop, Yum Chum Churros. `STRATEGY.md` says to ask five businesses at
