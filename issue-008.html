Hey PE 👋

It's Workers' Day long weekend and PE is packed. Dan Patlansky is recording a live album at The Music Kitchen, there's a boerewors braai at the SAAF Museum, night markets, musical theatre at Nineteen33, and three Mother's Day markets on Sunday to spoil Mom. Something for everyone.

Let's get into it.
