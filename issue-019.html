Hey PE 👋

August rolls in with Women's Month, and PE is leaning in — starting with a big Women's Month challenge at Baywest on Saturday morning. It’s a market-heavy weekend too, but not the usual: one is set inside the Air Force Museum (yes, really), and another is a bohemian one out on a farm. Plus a charity biker ride rumbling out from Cape Road.

Let's get into it.

★ Editor's Pick · Saturday

SAAF Museum Market Day

Sat 1 Aug · 11AM · SAAF Museum, Forest Hill · Free entry

A market set inside the Air Force Museum — food stalls, shopping, and lucky draws, with vintage aircraft as your backdrop. We loved the after-hours museum night a few weeks back; this is the bring-the-family daytime version. Where else do you browse for banana bread under a fighter jet?
