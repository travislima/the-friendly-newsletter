Welcome to the crew ●

I started The Friendly because I was tired of jumping between Facebook groups, WhatsApp chats, and Instagram just to figure out what's happening in PE this weekend.

So I thought — what if someone just curated it all into one short, punchy email? A weekly snapshot of the cool and interesting things to do in and around the city. No noise, no doom, just the good stuff.

That's what you'll get every Thursday at 8am — events, food, hidden gems, and whatever else makes this city the best-kept secret on the coast.
