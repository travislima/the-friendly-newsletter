Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

What we collect

When you subscribe to The Friendly, we collect your email address. That's it.

How we use it

We use your email address to send you our weekly newsletter every Thursday. We may occasionally send important updates about the newsletter itself. We will never send you spam or sell your information.

Email service

We use MailerLite to manage our mailing list and send emails. When you subscribe, your email address is stored on MailerLite's servers. MailerLite may collect basic analytics such as whether you opened an email or clicked a link.

No tracking on this site

We don't use analytics or advertising trackers on thefriendly.co.za. Our signup form is powered by MailerLite, whose embedded script may set a functional cookie to make the form work. That's the only third-party script on the site.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email. If you'd like us to delete your data entirely, email us at hello@thefriendly.co.za and we'll sort it out.

Contact

Questions? Reach us at hello@thefriendly.co.za.
