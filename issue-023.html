Hey PE 👋

Three markets in three days, which is a lot even by PE standards. Friday night at Fairview under the lights, then Sunday morning hands you two at once: the Re-Seconds turning ten, and a shelter fundraiser with a rescue-dog show attached. Both of the Sunday ones are happy to see your dog. Saturday meanwhile has a scavenger hunt through a wildlife sanctuary, which is not a sentence we get to write often.

Let's get into it.

★ Editor's Pick · Sunday

Save-a-Pet Market & Mini Fun Dog Show

Sunday 30 August · 9:30AM–2PM · Save-a-Pet Shelter, Old Cape Road, Greenbushes · R20 at the gate, under-10s free

A market at the shelter, with a mini fun dog show and the rescue dogs themselves doing most of the entertaining. R20 at the gate and under-tens free, which makes it the cheapest morning out this weekend by some distance. It is the shelter’s own fundraiser, so the money stays exactly where you would want it to.

🦏 Saturday
🦏

Forever Rhino Rally Junior Amazing Adventure Race

10AM–4PM · African Dawn Bird and Wildlife Sanctuary · Teams of 3 to 5, ages 5 and up

A scavenger hunt through the sanctuary, clue by clue, with a task to complete at every stop before the next one unlocks. At least one parent per team, prizes at the end, and vendors on site while you wait.

Build your team →
🎪 Sunday
🧺

The Re-Seconds Market turns 10

9AM–2PM · Walmer Town Hall, Cnr 8th Avenue and Main Road · Free entry

Ten years old this Sunday, and still the best place in the Bay for thrift, collectibles and other people’s good furniture. Portuguese and Chinese food stalls, food trucks, coffee and pancakes. Dogs welcome on a lead, and bring your own bag.
