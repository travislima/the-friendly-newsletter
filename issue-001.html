Worth Checking Out
 
New Opening

Ocean Brew Cafe opens this Saturday in Summerstrand

48 Erasmus Drive. Drive-thru and sit-down. Open 7 days. They've also got a kids zone, free wifi, a student corner, and a pensioners special every Tuesday (R50 for a hot drink and bakery item). They're even launching a running club on opening day at 5:30AM for the early birds.

Worth Knowing

One Cool Thing
 

The Plantery PE is transforming a rooftop in PE.

If you've walked through Moffett on Main Lifestyle Centre lately, you might have noticed the rooftop looking a little different. That's The Plantery PE. They're in the middle of a full rooftop revamp, turning a plain space into something green and beautiful. They're documenting the whole thing on Instagram in a three part series. Worth a follow if you're into plants, design, or just watching PE spaces get better.

@the_plantery_pe on Instagram →
