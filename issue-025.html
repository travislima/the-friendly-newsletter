★ Editor's Pick · Saturday

The Medieval Fayre & Banquet: Ode to the Bard

Saturday 19 September · 10AM–4PM · Hellenic Hall, Parsons Hill · R50pp, or R20 in costume

Over fifty market stalls, food trucks, pony rides, and actors doing Shakespeare at you from behind a hay bale. Entry is R50, or R20 if you arrive in medieval or Greek dress, which makes the costume box worth raiding. If you want to make a night of it, the banquet at 7PM is R250 and covers the meal, a Shakespearean production and your daytime entry. Take cash, as you would for any market.
