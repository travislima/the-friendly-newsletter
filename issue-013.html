★ Editor's Pick · All Weekend

Algoa Bay Whale Festival

Sat 6 & Sun 7 June · The Willows Resort, Marine Dr, Schoenmakerskop

The humpback whales are migrating and PE is celebrating. Day 1 is educational talks and conservation exhibits. Day 2 is full festival mode. Run by Raggy Charters, hosted at The Willows. This is uniquely PE and genuinely worth the drive.
