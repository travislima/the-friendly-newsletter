★ Editor's Pick · Sunday

Crossways Village Market

Sunday 23 August · 9AM–2PM · Crossways Farm Village, Castle Ridge Drive · Free entry

Food trucks, farmers, foodie artisans, crafters and flower vendors spread out under the blue gum trees, with Glen Webster playing and the mountains behind. Pony rides and face painting for the kids, dogs welcome on a lead, and it is a Women’s Month edition so wear pink if you feel like it. Yes it is a drive: exit 707 off the N2, just past Van Stadens Bridge. Worth the petrol.
