Hey PE 👋

This weekend is not messing around. New Orleans lands in Walmer with a full Mardi Gras carnival, Spoegwolf plays The Music Kitchen, and Deon Meyer (yes, THE Deon Meyer) shares a stage with Coenie de Villiers at NMU. Plus Christmas in July happens twice: a festive feast at Elephant Walk on Saturday and a second-chance Christmas market at Pari Park on Sunday.

Let's get into it.

Prefer reading this on WhatsApp? — join our group.
 
 

★ Editor's Pick · Saturday

Mardi Gras Carnival

Sat 25 July · 11AM till late · Bloomingdales Lifestyle Village, 145 Main Rd, Walmer

New Orleans comes to Walmer. An authentic four-piece jazz band, street performers, Brenton & Co, Devon Wade, food stalls, a market, and a fully packed kids zone. Over 400 people already interested. Tables at Tapas book up fast, so don’t sit on this one.

BCRA Boerewors Roll Fundraiser

cnr Buffelsfontein & Milner, Mount Pleasant · R30/roll

SA’s biggest crime writer and a music legend, one stage. The award-winning Karoo Suite series live, with a selection of Karoo stories and music.
