Hey PE 👋

Autumn vibes, mild weather, and a weekend that’s weirdly creative. Cabaret on Friday, pottery and lino printing on Saturday morning, a silent book club in the afternoon, and Bongeziwe Mabandla at The Music Kitchen that evening. Sunday brings Italian cars and a market in Mount Pleasant. Plus orchids all weekend at Walmer Park.

Let's get into it.

Prefer reading this on WhatsApp? — join our group.
 
 

★ Editor's Pick

Oskars Gelato

16 Walker Drive, Sherwood Garden Centre

Handcrafted gelato made with local ingredients. The ice cream is superb, and the waffles aren’t bad either. If you haven’t been yet, this is your sign.
