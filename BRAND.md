## What The Friendly is

A free weekly email newsletter about events, food, and things to do in
**Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha), South Africa**. Warm, local, in-the-know — like a
friend who always knows what's on this weekend. Upbeat, playful, never corporate.

---

## Logo / wordmark

- The word **FRIENDLY** set in **Archivo Black**, ALL CAPS, heavy weight,
  tight negative letter-spacing (about −2), dark ink `#1a1a1a`.
- A small **orange dot** (`#FF6B35`, a filled circle) sits immediately after the
  word — this dot is the signature device. Think "FRIENDLY●".
- Often preceded by a small, light, wide-spaced label **"THE"** above it.
- On dark backgrounds the wordmark is cream (`#FFFBF0`) with the same orange dot.

## Signature device: the four-colour gradient bar

A thin horizontal strip running left→right through four brand colours in this order:

`#FF6B35` (orange) → `#FFD23F` (yellow) → `#2EC4B6` (teal) → `#EE4266` (pink)

Used as a top or bottom edge accent. A sliver of this instantly reads as The Friendly.

## Visual style for imagery

- **Mood:** warm, sunny, welcoming, community, coastal PE energy. Feel-good, no doom.
- **Palette in images:** lean on cream, orange, and the four gradient colours.
  Warm and bright over cool and moody.
- **Subjects:** local PE life — markets, food, live music, beaches, families,
  weekend outings, the coastline. Real, un-staged, approachable.
- **Composition:** clean, uncluttered, generous negative space (cream works well
  as breathing room). Bold, simple, legible — designed to pop in a social feed.
- **Accents:** the orange dot and/or the four-colour gradient strip as a finishing touch.

### Avoid
- Corporate / stock-photo stiffness, grey or cold palettes, cluttered busy layouts,
  heavy drop shadows, anything that feels salesy or hype-y.

## Voice (for captions)

Warm, local, first-person-plural ("we"). Playful but never try-hard. Short punchy
sentences. Lead with the most interesting thing. "PE" casually. No hype words, no
negativity, no em-dashes in headings. Opens warm ("Hey PE 👋"), closes warm
("That's your weekend sorted. 🧡").
