School holidays are here and PE said “hold my coffee.” Saturday alone has pottery, cookie decorating, Vespa’s 80th birthday, a silent book club for charity, jazz at the harbour, AND a night at the actual Air Force Museum with curry and a torch. ★ Editor's Pick · Saturday

Night at the SAAF Museum

Sat 27 June · 7PM–10:30PM · SAAF Museum, Forest Hill Drive · R100pp

After-hours adventure surrounded by aviation history. Limited to 60 visitors. Includes curry, rice, and a glass of sherry. Bring warm clothes and a torch. This is genuinely one of the coolest things we’ve seen in PE. Booking essential: 079 600 6216.
