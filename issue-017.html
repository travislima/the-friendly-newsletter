Hey PE 👋

Mandela Day weekend and PE is bringing it. Friday alone has a mohair workshop with actual angora goats, kids crafts at The Harbour Master, ABBA hits at Hennie’s, and music bingo for a good cause. Saturday is Mandela Day itself, with a big community drive at Buffelsfontein Village, plus a market at Framesby, a kids rock show, and MORE ABBA (this time with a rugby build-up).

Let's get into it.
