Hey PE 👋

Women’s Day falls on Sunday, so Monday is a public holiday. Three days off instead of two, and PE has quietly filled all of them: four markets, a national racing festival at Scribante, and someone has turned the flying club into a laser tag battlefield for one night only.

Let's get into it.

★ Editor's Pick · Friday

Laser Tag: Epic Challenge

Fri 7 Aug · 6PM–8PM · Algoa Flying Club, Chief Dawid Stuurman Airport · R180pp

The flying club grounds get turned into a laser tag battlefield for one night, bar open and fires going. You pick a character before you start: trooper, sniper, medic, hostage, vampire or zombie, and a live stats system ranks everyone at the end. It runs once a year, it works for a group of mates or a family, and it is firmly in the category of things you did not know you could do in PE.
