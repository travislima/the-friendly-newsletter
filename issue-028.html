★ Editor's Pick · Sunday

The Algoa Cup: Festival of Racing

Sun 18 Oct · Gates 11:30AM · Fairview Racecourse, Draaifontein Road, Greenbushes · Free entry

PE's biggest race day of the year, and it costs nothing to walk in: the Algoa Cup on the track, a market and beer tent beside it, a kids' play area, and the tote on course if you fancy a flutter. Sunday best is encouraged. It runs rain or shine, the market sits under the grandstand and the track is all-weather, which is more than any other market this weekend can promise. The only thing that needs booking is the Bold Silvano room (buffet, welcome drink, betting voucher): email gailr@4racing.com or call 083 390 0968.

Framesby Market at Smeeton Park

9AM–2PM · Smeeton Road, Framesby · Bring cash

A new one: local vendors, food and crafts in the park, run by the residents. Stall fees go towards CCTV for the suburb, so every koeksister is a camera.

Crossways Village Market

9AM–2PM · Crossways Farm Village, exit 707 off the N2 · Free entry

Rained off two weeks ago, back under the blue gums this Sunday: food trucks, lifestyle stalls, pony rides and live music from Claire North and Russel Sneyd. Weather permitting, as always.

Filthys Monthly Market

12PM–5PM · Filthys, 55 Main Road, Walmer · Free entry

Fresh produce, food stalls, artisan goods, plants and a thrift rail from ThriftGQ, all in the Filthys courtyard. Starts at noon, so it slots in after the morning markets.
